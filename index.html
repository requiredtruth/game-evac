MISSION BRIEF

Find. Protect. Extract.

Search houses, shops and chapels. Escort survivors to the marked landing zone. Each person requires two game minutes of helicopter winch time.

Two turret-equipped Hummers await at base. Enter civilian cars, scavenge ammunition, throw red flares for air support and call in supply drops.

Darkness makes the infected faster and more aggressive. Leave the city before the clock reaches zero. Nobody left inside survives.
